# Weekly review

1. `list_tasks` (open tasks) and `list_events` for Monday to Sunday.
2. For each day, free hours with `free_slots` (05:30-22:30, Alex's focus times are early morning and after 20:30).
3. Compare: hours needed before each deadline vs free hours before it. Flag every deadline that doesn't fit.
4. Name the crunch day (least free time, most due).
5. Suggest at most three things to move, shorten or drop. Low-priority tasks go first. Never suggest dropping
   the gym or Saturday with the kids.
6. Answer as: Deadlines at risk · Crunch day · Three suggestions. Under 150 words.
